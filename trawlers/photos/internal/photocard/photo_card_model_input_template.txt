# What to write
Answer in plain text with exactly these four headings, in this order, nothing before or after:

## Summary
One specific sentence describing what the photo shows.

## Description
Thorough, concrete prose so someone who cannot see the photo can picture the scene, people, actions, setting, lighting, weather and notable details. Describe what is in the picture; do not repeat coordinates or camera settings.

## Visible text
Readable text in the picture, grouped by location with "### <where>" headings, exact script and line breaks, and at most one "English: ..." line at the end of a non-English group. Use "[?]" for an unreadable character inside a readable line, and omit labels that cannot be read at all (mention them in Description instead). Write "None." if there is no readable text.

## Place shown
Two lines:
Name: the most specific place the photo actually shows followed by its wider area, or "None" if it shows no place (such as a screenshot), or "Unknown" if the surroundings cannot be identified.
Reasoning: one sentence explaining the evidence.
