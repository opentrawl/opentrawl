# OpenTrawl 0.1.16

## Fixes

- **Twitter search role labels:** Label every Twitter search match by role (`Authored`, `Repost`, `Bookmark`, `Mention`, `Like`, or `Direct message`) so liked posts no longer blend into what you wrote.
- **Apple Notes table rendering:** Render captured Apple Notes tables directly when opening notes, replace the broken `trawl notes sync` placeholder with `trawl update notes`, and refresh stored note bodies and full-text search entries once table data is captured.
- **Deduplicated X DM participants:** Stop printing participants twice in opened X direct message headers and conversation participant lists.
- **All-time Twitter stats and clearer `--window` help:** Document accepted `--window` values in `trawl twitter stats --help`, support `all`, weeks (`w`), and years (`y`), and exclude retweets (`RT @…`) from personal engagement rankings so stats reflect your own posts.
- **Accurate Gmail and Apple Mail date cutoffs:** Exclude undated messages from bounded `--before` and `--after` searches, and fall back cleanly between Gmail's `internalDate` and RFC 5322 `Date` headers when importing or reparsing messages.
- **More consistent links and formatting:** Unify short-reference link management and Money search/detail formatting on shared OpenTrawl core surfaces across the CLI and Mac app.
