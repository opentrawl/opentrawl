Non-terminal streams and coding-agent harnesses now render tables and opened
records at unconstrained natural width instead of narrowing to 80 or 100
columns, while explicit `COLUMNS` overrides terminal width and interactive human
terminals keep their exact window width. Added hidden local complaint logging
(`trawl complain`, `--complaint`, and `trawl complaints`) backed by
`~/.opentrawl/complaints.jsonl` so agents can record reproducible search gaps
and UX friction without cluttering the human CLI.
