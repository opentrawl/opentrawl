# OpenTrawl 0.1.15

## Fixes

- **Compact non-TTY output and bounded search snippets:** Stop padding wide table columns across every row in unconstrained non-TTY output, and bound search result match fields to match-centred snippets so long OCR text or messages do not swamp search tables.
- **Cleaner Gmail and Apple Mail text:** Detect HTML documents embedded in `text/plain` parts, strip literal HTML preheader spacer entities such as `&#8199;&#847;`, and bump the email parser revision so existing archives re-parse cleanly on the next update.
- **Readable WhatsApp 1-on-1 counterparties:** Strip Unicode formatting marks and fall back to readable phone numbers or `@handles` when no saved contact name exists, without leaking internal `@lid` identifiers.
- **Cleaner person lookup and cross-source matching:** Stop `trawl who` from fuzzy-matching internal WhatsApp `@lid` identifiers, merge `@googlemail.com` with `@gmail.com` in Contacts, strip European trunk zeroes in iMessage phone normalization, and union split Contacts records when filtering by an exact email address or phone number.
- **Telegram supergroup authorship:** Fix Postbox `ThreadId` decoding so supergroup messages keep their real sender, direction, and media type, and allow repaired sender facts to heal previously affected rows on update.
- **ING booked history coverage:** Record complete booked history intervals for ING after full Enable Banking paging (matching Wise) and bridge any gap from the previous complete end date on the next read.
- **Consistent date-range flags, status timestamps, and domain search:** Use `--after` and `--before` consistently across `photos days`, `messages`, `notes`, and `calendar events`; report `newest record` in `trawl status` for Contacts and Calendar; support date-bounded Contacts searches; and phrase-match dotted domain names such as `Booking.com` in full-text search.
