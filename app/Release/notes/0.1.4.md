# OpenTrawl Alpha 0.1.4

Your financial history can now join your messages, notes and other archives in OpenTrawl. Money imports accounts, dated balances, booked transactions and pending activity from completed financial downloads. Search payments alongside your other records, open their details, or list transactions for one account and matching text. History coverage makes gaps visible before you draw a conclusion from a missing payment. An existing Enable Banking connection can refresh at most once every 24 hours; other imports come from an agent or another collector. Money does not sign in to banks or card websites.

Photos now searches and opens facts already held by Apple Photos: capture dates, places, named people, albums, captions, camera details and text Apple has recognised. You can also browse photos and their local days. This Alpha reads metadata only: fetching pictures, looking up nearby places and generating descriptions are not available yet.

CLI tables stay compact on wide displays and use extra space for text that needs it. Times show their UTC offsets, with UTC added in full details. Help, errors and next commands follow the same conventions across sources.

Search selects the newest matches before applying its result limit. Opening a note shows its complete text and explains when it is empty. Note searches and date filters use the note's modified date. Message counts agree with searches involving the same person, conversations use their newest archived message time, and calendar links open their calendar details.
