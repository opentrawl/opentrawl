# OpenTrawl Alpha 0.1.4

Your financial history can now join your messages, notes and other archives in OpenTrawl. Money imports accounts, balances and transactions from completed financial downloads, with an optional daily read through an existing Enable Banking connection.

Search payments alongside your other records, open their full details, or list transactions for one account and matching text. Account balances show their source dates or read times, and history coverage makes gaps visible before you draw a conclusion from a missing payment.

Money does not sign in to banks or card websites. An agent or another collector must supply financial imports; Enable Banking needs an existing consent and local configuration.
