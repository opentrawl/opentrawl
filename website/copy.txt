[connect.title]
Connect your AI in one step

[connect.lead]
Download the Mac app, let it build your local archive, then paste this one prompt into Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes, Cursor, T3 Code, or any local coding agent on your Mac.

[connect.prompt.label]
One-shot setup prompt

[connect.prompt.text]
Use my local OpenTrawl archive at `/Applications/OpenTrawl.app/Contents/Helpers/trawl`. Start by running it with no arguments and with `--help`, then run a small read-only search and open one result to show me how it works. Once you have shown me the demo, ask me what I think and whether I want you to use OpenTrawl permanently (for example by adding a short note to my `AGENTS.md`). Do not run `update`, install plugins, or change any files unless I ask.

[connect.prompt.note]
Works today with any agent that runs terminal commands, including agents powered by local models (Ollama or MLX).

[wiki.title]
Build a personal wiki from your history in one shot

[wiki.lead]
Most AI memory waits months to learn who you are and traps that memory inside one company's chat app. OpenTrawl already has years of your messages, email, notes, and calendar on your Mac. Run this once to generate a Markdown wiki your agents can read on Day 1.

[wiki.prompt.label]
One-shot personal wiki prompt

[wiki.prompt.text]
Using my local OpenTrawl archive at `/Applications/OpenTrawl.app/Contents/Helpers/trawl`, inspect my history across messages, email, notes, and calendar (starting with the last 12 months). Build a Markdown personal wiki in `~/wiki/` organised into `people/`, `projects/`, `home/`, `finances/`, `trips/`, and `open-matters.md`. Cite every fact with its exact `trawl open` command so I can verify anything directly.

[wiki.prompt.note]
Re-run weekly to fold new messages, emails, and notes into the same Markdown files.

[wiki.output.label]
What your AI builds in ~/wiki/

[wiki.output.note]
Plain Markdown files you own in Obsidian or git. Switch AI models tomorrow and keep your entire personal memory.

[privacy.title]
Privacy is a human right

[privacy.lead]
Your messages, finances, and photos belong to you. OpenTrawl is built in the EU 🇪🇺, open source under MIT, and engineered so we can never read your data: local on your Mac today, and end-to-end encrypted in our planned sync and cloud tiers.

[privacy.p1.title]
Everything stays on your Mac

[privacy.p1.body]
Your archives are plain local files in `~/.opentrawl`. No OpenTrawl servers, no account, no analytics, and no tracking.

[privacy.p2.title]
Run with local models so no AI company sees your data

[privacy.p2.body]
Point a local coding agent running Ollama or MLX on Apple Silicon at OpenTrawl and neither OpenTrawl nor any AI company sees your messages, emails, or notes.

[privacy.p3.title]
End-to-end encrypted sync & cloud (planned)

[privacy.p3.body]
On our planned sync and cloud tiers, your archive is encrypted on your device before upload. Cloud search runs inside hardware-locked memory that requires your live key, so even OpenTrawl cannot read your data.

[privacy.p4.title]
Read-only, never sends or edits

[privacy.p4.body]
OpenTrawl only copies your history for search. It cannot send a message, delete an email, or edit a note.

[privacy.fda.title]
Have your AI check that it does what it says it does

[privacy.fda.body]
Reading Apple apps like Messages, Notes, and Mail requires macOS Full Disk Access. Don't take our word for it: open the Mac app and use **Click to copy AI review prompt** so Claude or Codex can inspect the app bundle on your Mac against our public GitHub source before you turn it on.
