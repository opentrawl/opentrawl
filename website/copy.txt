[hero.sub]
OpenTrawl is a macOS app and command-line tool. It builds a private, searchable local archive of your messages, email, notes, photos, calendar, contacts, health, finances, and X history so you and any AI can search them together.

[hero.privacy]
Nothing leaves your machine, unless you want it to.

[sources.today]
**12 built-in sources:** iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, Apple Mail, Gmail, Notes, Contacts, Calendar, Photos, Apple Health, Money, and Twitter (X).

[sources.soon]
**Plus sandboxed plugins & scoped MCP:** add any other app or export with `trawl plugin install`, or connect Claude, Codex, Cursor, OpenClaw, Hermes, and T3 Code with `trawl connect`.

[terminal.caption]
I took my real archive and told Claude to anonymise it by LARPing as The
Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. The data is real, the names aren't.

[fact.1.title]
Private by design

[fact.1.body]
Your archives are just files on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, we don't know who you are.
If you want to point a (local) AI at your archives, *you're* in control - not a company.

[fact.2.title]
It just works

[fact.2.body]
Install the Mac app. It builds local SQLite archives and unified search indexes, keeps them updated, and connects to your AI via CLI or scoped read-only MCP (`trawl connect`).

[fact.3.title]
Open source

[fact.3.body]
The code is on GitHub, MIT licensed. Take a look, see how it works. No secrets here.

[fact.4.title]
Sandboxed plugins

[fact.4.body]
Want to search something we have not built yet? Hand our [plugin skill](https://github.com/opentrawl/opentrawl/blob/main/skills/build-trawl-plugin/SKILL.md) to your coding agent and install it under strict macOS sandboxing with `trawl plugin install`.
