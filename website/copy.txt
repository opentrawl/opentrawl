[wiki.title]
Build your personal wiki on Day 1

[wiki.lead]
Most AI memory waits months to learn who you are and traps that memory inside one chat app. OpenTrawl already has years of your messages, email, notes, and calendar on your Mac. Point your agent at `trawl` once and it compiles a plain Markdown personal wiki with links back to every source record.

[wiki.prompt.label]
One-shot prompt for your AI

[wiki.prompt.text]
Use my local OpenTrawl archive via `/Applications/OpenTrawl.app/Contents/Helpers/trawl`. Search my last 12 months across messages, email, notes, and calendar, then build a Markdown personal wiki in `~/wiki/` covering key people, active projects, commitments, and decisions. Cite every fact with its exact `trawl open` link.

[wiki.prompt.note]
Re-run weekly as new messages and emails arrive. Your archives update in the background.

[wiki.output.label]
What your agent writes in ~/wiki/

[wiki.output.note]
Plain Markdown files you own in Obsidian or git. Switch AI models tomorrow and keep your whole wiki.

[connect.title]
Connect the AI you already use

[connect.lead]
Works with **Claude Code**, **Claude Desktop**, **Codex**, **OpenClaw**, **Hermes**, **Cursor**, **T3 Code**, and **executor.sh**. Start by pasting one prompt, or run `trawl connect` to preview exact config changes and choose which sources each agent can read.

[connect.prompt.label]
Paste into any AI that can run tools

[connect.prompt.text]
Use my local OpenTrawl archive at `/Applications/OpenTrawl.app/Contents/Helpers/trawl`. Start by running it with no arguments and with `--help`, then run a small read-only search and open one result to show me how it works. Once you have shown me the demo, ask me what I think and whether I want you to use OpenTrawl more often (for example by adding a short instruction to my `AGENTS.md` or setting up a skill). Do not run `update`, install plugins, or change any files or configuration unless I ask.

[connect.cli.label]
Or connect installed agents in one step

[connect.cli.note]
`trawl connect` detects installed local agents, shows the exact config diff before touching any file, and lets you restrict an agent to specific sources (for example Notes and Calendar only).

[privacy.title]
Private by design

[privacy.p1.title]
Your data stays on your Mac

[privacy.p1.body]
Your archives are plain files in `~/.opentrawl`. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and we do not know who you are.

[privacy.p2.title]
No servers, no account, no tracking

[privacy.p2.body]
OpenTrawl runs locally with zero telemetry. The code is on GitHub under the MIT licence. No secrets here.

[privacy.p3.title]
Read-only

[privacy.p3.body]
OpenTrawl copies your history so you and your AI can search it. It never sends messages, edits notes, or writes back to your apps.

[privacy.p4.title]
Your AI sees only what it opens

[privacy.p4.body]
Whatever records an agent reads go to your AI provider under its privacy terms. Limit exposure by granting each agent only the sources it needs.

[privacy.fda.title]
Check the app before granting Full Disk Access

[privacy.fda.body]
Reading Apple apps like Messages, Notes, and Mail on macOS requires Full Disk Access. Before you turn it on, click **Audit this build** inside the Mac app to have Claude or Codex inspect the exact signed app bundle on your disk against the public GitHub commit.
