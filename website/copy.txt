[connect.title]
Connect your AI in one step

[connect.lead]
Download the Mac app, let it build your local archive, then paste this one prompt into Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes, Cursor, T3 Code, or a local model on your Mac.

[connect.prompt.label]
One-shot setup prompt

[connect.prompt.text]
Use my local OpenTrawl archive at `/Applications/OpenTrawl.app/Contents/Helpers/trawl`. Start by running it with no arguments and with `--help`, then run a small read-only search and open one result to show me how it works. Once you have shown me the demo, ask me what I think and whether I want you to use OpenTrawl permanently (for example by adding a short note to my `AGENTS.md` or setting up a skill). Do not run `update`, install plugins, or change any files unless I ask.

[connect.prompt.note]
Works today with any agent that can run commands, including local models via Ollama or MLX.

[wiki.title]
Build a personal wiki from your whole history in one shot

[wiki.lead]
Most AI memory waits months to learn who you are and traps that memory inside one company's chat app. OpenTrawl already has years of your messages, email, notes, calendar, and receipts on your Mac. Run this once to generate a clean, linked Markdown wiki your agents can read on Day 1.

[wiki.prompt.label]
One-shot personal wiki prompt

[wiki.prompt.text]
Using my local OpenTrawl archive at `/Applications/OpenTrawl.app/Contents/Helpers/trawl`, inspect the last 12 months across messages, email, notes, calendar, and money. Build a Markdown personal wiki in `~/wiki/` organised into `people/`, `projects/`, `home/`, `finances/`, `trips/`, and `open-matters.md`. Cite every fact with its exact `trawl open` link so I can verify anything in one click.

[wiki.prompt.note]
Re-run weekly to fold new messages, emails, and receipts into the same Markdown files.

[privacy.title]
Privacy is a human right

[privacy.lead]
Your messages, finances, health, and photos belong to you. OpenTrawl is built in the EU 🇪🇺, open source under MIT, and engineered so we can never read your data — on your Mac today, and in our planned encrypted cloud tiers.

[privacy.p1.title]
Everything stays on your Mac

[privacy.p1.body]
Your archives are plain local files in `~/.opentrawl`. No OpenTrawl servers, no account, no analytics, and no tracking.

[privacy.p2.title]
Run with local models for zero cloud contact

[privacy.p2.body]
Point a local model on Apple Silicon (Ollama or MLX) at OpenTrawl and neither OpenTrawl nor any AI company ever sees a single message, email, or note.

[privacy.p3.title]
End-to-end encrypted by default

[privacy.p3.body]
On our planned sync and cloud tiers, your archive is encrypted on your device before upload. Cloud search runs inside hardware-locked confidential memory that requires your live key, so even OpenTrawl cannot see your data.

[privacy.p4.title]
Read-only, never sends or edits

[privacy.p4.body]
OpenTrawl only copies your history for search. It cannot send a message, delete an email, or edit a note.

[privacy.fda.title]
Have your AI check that it does what it says it does

[privacy.fda.body]
Reading Apple apps like Messages, Notes, and Mail requires macOS Full Disk Access. You should never trust a privacy claim on faith: open the Mac app and click **Click to copy AI review prompt** to let Claude or Codex inspect the exact app bundle on your disk against our public GitHub source before you turn it on.
