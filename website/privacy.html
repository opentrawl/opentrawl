Gmail privacy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

The OpenTrawl app runs on your Mac. When you connect Gmail, the app communicates directly with Google and stores your archive on your Mac.

The OpenTrawl project does not operate a Gmail backend. Its maintainers receive no Google account identity, messages, connection events, searches, archive statistics, diagnostics or telemetry. We do not know who has connected Gmail and cannot remotely access their archives.

OpenTrawl's source code is public. You can inspect the code that connects to Google, downloads messages, stores the archive and protects the Google credential.

Connecting Gmail

The app asks Google for read-only access to Gmail. It uses this access to build and update your local archive.

The connection is between the app on your Mac and Google. It does not pass through an OpenTrawl server.

The app cannot send, change or delete email. It never receives your Google password.

Data stored on your Mac

The archive contains message text and information such as subjects, senders, recipients, dates and labels. It also contains attachment names, types and sizes.

The app does not currently download attachment contents or HTML versions of messages.

The archive is stored at ~/.opentrawl/gmail/gmail.db.

Your stored Gmail data never leaves your Mac through OpenTrawl. Data leaves your Mac only if you deliberately open, export or share it with another tool or service.

Your Google credential

The app stores the credential used to maintain its Google connection in the protected macOS Keychain. The credential is bound to your Mac and can only be used by signed OpenTrawl software.

Temporary Google access tokens remain in memory while the app is running. Credentials and access tokens are never sent to the OpenTrawl project or its maintainers.

Retention and deletion

Your archive remains on your Mac until you delete it.

Disconnecting Gmail revokes the app's Google access and removes its stored credential. It does not delete the local archive. Messages already downloaded may remain in the archive after they are deleted from Gmail.

To delete the archive, open ~/.opentrawl/gmail/ in Finder and move gmail.db to the Bin.

You can also revoke the app's access from your Google Account.

Sharing and advertising

The OpenTrawl project cannot inspect, sell or share your Gmail data because it never receives that data.

The app does not use Gmail data for advertising, tracking or training shared AI models.

Contact

Questions about Gmail data and privacy can be sent to privacy@opentrawl.sh.

Google API data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
