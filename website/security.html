Security & trust boundaries

Today OpenTrawl runs 100% locally on your Mac with no servers, no accounts, and no telemetry. As we add optional encrypted sync and hosted search, you choose the exact boundary your data lives in.

What we cannot hide

  • Your AI provider sees what your agent reads. When you let Claude, Codex, or another model open a conversation or email, that text goes to your AI provider under its privacy terms. Use trawl connect --sources ... to give each agent only the sources it needs.
  • Cloud storage sees coarse metadata. On planned cloud tiers, storage servers cannot read your archive contents or source names, but they can still see padded file sizes and sync timestamps. Stay on Local if your threat model allows zero cloud contact.
  • Compromised devices. If malware is running on your Mac or iPhone, local files and unlocked sessions are exposed.

Planned verifiable cloud builds

Status: Planned architecture (not live yet). When Cloud Vault ships, every enclave image will be built reproducibly from public GitHub commits, logged in a public 72-hour upgrade window before running enclaves accept it, and checkable from your terminal so you do not have to take our word for what is running on our servers.

Audit the Mac app today

OpenTrawl is MIT open source on GitHub. Inside the Mac app's setup flow, click Audit this build to copy a prompt pinned to the exact app bundle on your disk and its Git commit, then paste it into Claude or Codex before granting Full Disk Access.
